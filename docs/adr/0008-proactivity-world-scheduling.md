# ADR-0008: Proactivity, World Life and Scheduling

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-01

## Decision

These are related but distinct:
- World owns fictional character life/routine/story truth.
- Proactivity decides whether/when a user-facing initiative is appropriate.
- Workflows/workers own durable scheduled execution.
- Social owns posting cadence/review policy.

N.E.K.O donates proactive-domain decomposition/persistence.
OpenJarvis donates generic once/interval/cron semantics.
ChatWithStars donates world/life idempotency and social scheduling.

No single assistant scheduler becomes the source of all temporal truth.

# Masanawa Vision

Status: **current product vision — 2026-10-07**

Masanawa is building a new kind of social network around **live human conversation
enhanced by AI**.

A real person can start a public audio Talk about almost anything, invite other people,
bring specialist Masanawa Experts or user-owned Twins into limited Seats, and build an
audience around the quality of the conversation.

Listeners are not passive. They can react, raise a hand, ask questions, gift the host,
sponsor an AI participant, bring/request a Twin and follow the people/personas they enjoy.

## What makes Masanawa different

The AI is not the content owner. The human is.

Masanawa AI should help ordinary people host compelling conversations by:
- asking useful follow-up questions;
- challenging weak arguments;
- explaining difficult ideas;
- moderating;
- fact-checking within capability;
- bringing specialist perspectives;
- keeping a conversation moving without replacing the human.

Every live public Talk therefore requires a real human Host. AI-only autonomous public
Talks are outside the product vision.

## Creator economy

The network creates economic value around:
- AI Seat fees;
- Twin rentals;
- gifts;
- later subscriptions;
- later sponsorship/advertising;
- later qualified-attention rewards.

A useful Twin becomes an owned AI persona other people may pay to bring to their table.
The owner can privately direct that Twin while it participates.

## Simple consumer shape

Primary navigation:

**For You · Live · Create · Activity · You**

The app should optimize for:
1. finding something worth listening to;
2. starting a Talk with almost no setup;
3. participating without friction;
4. following people/personas;
5. earning when audiences value the contribution.

The product is not organized around Rooms, Skills, Tools, agents or workflows.

## Private assistant

Gwani remains the private operating assistant for Masanawa. Users can ask Gwani for
research, teaching, explanation, writing, search, preparation, platform navigation and
other AI capabilities without creating dedicated consumer sections.

Private assistant activity never becomes public automatically.

## Standalone services

Only two utility families receive dedicated consumer service flows:
- Billing
- Crypto

See `CONSUMER-SERVICES.md`.

## Technical platform

Under the simple social product, Masanawa continues to use one canonical internal
platform for:
- identity;
- memory;
- agent runtime;
- Skills/Tools/Providers;
- live voice;
- channels;
- Commerce/ledger;
- security;
- recovery/audit.

These systems exist to make the consumer experience reliable. They are not the
consumer experience themselves.

## Long-term opportunity

If the product succeeds, Masanawa can become a global graph of:
- people worth listening to;
- conversations worth joining;
- AI personas worth hiring;
- expertise available on demand;
- creator-owned intellectual identities that can earn from appearances.

The long-term ambition is not “another AI assistant.”

It is:

> **A global live conversation network where human curiosity creates the show and AI
> makes the table more capable.**

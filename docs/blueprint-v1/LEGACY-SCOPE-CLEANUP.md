## Non-negotiable product rules
- **No personal Twins/clones** anywhere in onboarding, agent directory, profile, Chats, Talks, Settings, Wallet, billing, channels or admin. No user-created representative agent, teaching/training that personal agent, clone-sharing link, paid owner access, owner-session chat, live seats, creator credit, credit-based twin routing, creator earnings or commission/referral accounting for new operations. These are removed from product scope, **not moved to a later roadmap**.
- **Keep platform Gwani/Gwana/Masana** and their centrally administered skills/services, ordinary human contacts and human published Talks. Keep valid human-to-human chat, native voice notes, useful teaching by platform agents, purchases, wallet and connected approved channels.
- **No repetitive AI badges, "this is AI" bubbles, trust lectures, synthetic-content banner on every Talk or agent, or defensive marketing disclaimers.** Use normal names, avatars, roles and concise capabilities. Do not show unnecessary human-vs-AI labeling as design differentiation. Any independently established regulatory/provider-specific disclosure belongs only at the required point.
- Replace retired feature entry points with current legitimate destinations or a minimal safe historical-status page. Never leave a tappable dead-end, crash, broken deep link or inaccessible recorded receipt.
- All new feature implementations must follow this scope, including older unfinished PRs. Preserve existing transaction records, receipts, credits and outstanding disputes until formally settled; deleting a UI is not an accounting correction.
